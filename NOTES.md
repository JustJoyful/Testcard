## Findings

- **Extraction feasibility passed under the reduced spatial scope.** Hardware/pretrained checks supported proceeding; extraction projections were planning estimates, not measured total-project or API runtime. Sources: [results/phase0/report.md](results/phase0/report.md); [results/phase0/feasibility.json](results/phase0/feasibility.json); [results/phase0/scoped_feasibility.json](results/phase0/scoped_feasibility.json).
- **Representation mattered more than temporal-head choice on KoNViD under this protocol.** All same-head ResNet–DINO feature comparisons establish a practically meaningful ResNet advantage; no primary selected-feature head comparison establishes a practically meaningful advantage. This is a comparison of observed effects, not a formal representation-by-head interaction test. Feature comparisons are secondary and unadjusted. Sources: [results/phase2/feature_pairs.csv](results/phase2/feature_pairs.csv); [results/phase2/head_comparison.csv](results/phase2/head_comparison.csv).
- **No practically meaningful advantage demonstrated for a neural head, Mamba included, in the primary KoNViD or LIVE comparisons.** This does not establish equivalence. KoNViD includes detectable differences that do not establish an effect beyond the practical margin; LIVE primary pairs are inconclusive. Sources: [results/phase2/head_comparison.csv](results/phase2/head_comparison.csv); [results/phase3/comparisons.csv](results/phase3/comparisons.csv).
- **Transformer versus Mamba on KoNViD is detectable, not shown to exceed the margin.** This pair survives Holm adjustment; avoid saying that all heads are indistinguishable or that every statistical test was nonsignificant. Sources: [results/phase2/head_comparison.csv](results/phase2/head_comparison.csv).
- **Ridge is competitive.** It has the highest selected-representation KoNViD headline; paired ridge comparisons do not demonstrate a practically meaningful neural advantage. Its inconclusive comparisons do not establish equivalence. Sources: [results/phase2/headline.csv](results/phase2/headline.csv); [results/phase2/head_comparison.csv](results/phase2/head_comparison.csv); [results/phase3/comparisons.csv](results/phase3/comparisons.csv).
- **ResNet's KoNViD advantage is not confirmed on LIVE.** All LIVE feature-comparison intervals include zero. DINO ridge's numerical LIVE SRCC is 0.6584, versus ResNet ridge's 0.6439; the paired comparison is inconclusive, not a demonstrated reversal. Sources: [results/phase2/feature_pairs.csv](results/phase2/feature_pairs.csv); [results/phase3/headline.csv](results/phase3/headline.csv); [results/phase3/comparisons.csv](results/phase3/comparisons.csv).
- **Lower cross-dataset rank correlation.** ResNet headline SRCC declines by 0.1172–0.1375 across the reported heads/baseline. Ridge goes from 0.7801 on KoNViD to 0.6439 on LIVE. These are different estimands and training regimes; the difference cannot isolate domain shift. Sources: [results/phase2/headline.csv](results/phase2/headline.csv); [results/phase3/headline.csv](results/phase3/headline.csv); [results/phase3/report.md](results/phase3/report.md).
- **Backbone/extraction dominates CPU cost.** Selected ResNet + ridge reaches a maximum normalized raw-video-to-score time of 8.3278 seconds per ten-second video on 4 CPU threads. Ridge head time in that maximum row is 0.000128 seconds per ten-second video; backbone computation dominates the timed pipeline. One-time loading and the API path are excluded. Sources: [results/phase3/cpu_eligibility.json](results/phase3/cpu_eligibility.json).
- **ResNet + ridge is the deployment selection, not a completed service.** The winner was chosen using frozen KoNViD headline accuracy among CPU-eligible candidates before LIVE evaluation. Deterministic ridge uses its full-KoNViD artifact; deployment parity and API measurement remain pending. Sources: [results/phase3/deployment.json](results/phase3/deployment.json); [results/phase3/phase_gate.json](results/phase3/phase_gate.json); [results/phase3/report.md](results/phase3/report.md).

## Limitations

- **Selection/test residual overlap:** inner-validation and tuning videos can be held-out test videos in other outer folds; this is not an independent nested estimate of the entire selection process. Sources: [results/phase2/summary.json](results/phase2/summary.json); [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md); [RULES.md](RULES.md).
- **Hyperparameters tuned on DINO and reused on ResNet:** equal reuse avoids representation-specific retuning but may favor or under-tune architectures differently. Sources: [results/phase2/report.md](results/phase2/report.md); [results/phase2/effective_configs.json](results/phase2/effective_configs.json).
- **Short selected training schedules:** the selected ResNet median CV best epochs range from 3 to 8; mean pooling uses 3 while its warmup lasts 5 epochs, so the median mean-pool checkpoint lies inside warmup. This is not a claim that every CV fit peaks in that range. Sources: [results/phase2/konvid_freeze.json](results/phase2/konvid_freeze.json).
- **Ridge alpha reaches the search boundary:** the frozen ResNet full-refit alpha is 1000, the top of the declared grid; stronger regularization was not evaluated, so the optimum beyond the grid is unknown. Sources: [results/phase2/konvid_freeze.json](results/phase2/konvid_freeze.json).
- **Median-epoch refit approximation:** full-data fitting has more optimizer steps per epoch than CV, so transferring the median best epoch does not preserve the original update count or validate an optimal full-data stopping time. Sources: [results/phase2/konvid_freeze.json](results/phase2/konvid_freeze.json); [results/phase3/report.md](results/phase3/report.md).
- **Warning-only determinism:** seeds and deterministic settings are fixed, but nondeterministic-operation warnings do not abort; no bitwise reproducibility guarantee. Sources: [src/training.py](src/training.py); [results/phase1/report.md](results/phase1/report.md); [results/phase2/summary.json](results/phase2/summary.json).
- **Conditional bootstrap uncertainty:** paired intervals capture test-video sampling noise for seed-averaged predictions, not retraining, fold assignment or selection uncertainty; seed/fold variability is reported separately. Sources: [results/phase2/summary.json](results/phase2/summary.json); [results/phase3/summary.json](results/phase3/summary.json); [PRD.md](PRD.md).
- **Conservative equivalence criterion:** the declared two-sided 95% CI must lie inside the practical margin; this is more conservative for equivalence than the 90% CI used by a conventional TOST at the corresponding significance level. Inconclusive is not equivalent. Sources: [PRD.md](PRD.md); [results/phase2/comparisons.meta.json](results/phase2/comparisons.meta.json); [results/phase3/comparisons.meta.json](results/phase3/comparisons.meta.json).
- **Mapped PLCC uses target labels:** logistic fitting on evaluated targets makes PLCC a descriptive target-domain mapped metric, not label-free transfer calibration or the deployed score; SRCC/KRCC use raw predictions. Sources: [results/phase2/summary.json](results/phase2/summary.json); [results/phase3/summary.json](results/phase3/summary.json); [results/phase3/deployment.json](results/phase3/deployment.json).
- **Center crop and portraits:** center cropping discards peripheral content, especially on portrait videos; rotation handling does not recover cropped quality cues. Sources: [results/phase3/summary.json](results/phase3/summary.json); [results/phase0/rotation_review.json](results/phase0/rotation_review.json); [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md).
- **Sequence-length mismatch:** KoNViD cached sequences have median 33 steps, range 31–33; LIVE has median 41, range 39–41. Dataset and duration effects are confounded; no controlled length extrapolation test was run. Sources: [results/phase3/lengths.json](results/phase3/lengths.json).
- **Domain shift is not isolated:** datasets differ in content, distortions and duration while the training regime also changes; the observed headline drop cannot be attributed wholly to any one factor. Sources: [results/phase3/report.md](results/phase3/report.md); [results/phase3/lengths.json](results/phase3/lengths.json).
- **Bounded tuning and frozen backbones:** conclusions concern the cached spatial features and equal bounded search used here, not all possible architectures, end-to-end training or stronger hyperparameter searches. Sources: [results/phase2/report.md](results/phase2/report.md); [configs/scope.yaml](configs/scope.yaml); [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).

## 2026-10-31: Post-hoc exploratory analysis — why DINOv2 lost to ResNet-50 on KoNViD

**Post-hoc, exploratory, unadjusted.** Predeclared in DECISIONS.md (commit `448efe2`) before any computation; the append is pinned in [decision_append.json](results/posthoc_dino/decision_append.json) and checked by `verify_snapshot`. No frozen configuration, deployed model, phase gate or headline result changed; the deployed model remains the ResNet-50 ridge. All numbers are in the generated [report](results/posthoc_dino/report.md) and its CSV/JSON files; none are typed here.

- **Reproduction check:** the A0 variant (Phase 1/2 DINO features) reproduced the saved per-fold SRCC within the predeclared 1e-9 tolerance before any other variant was reported.
- **Part A finding:** under the predeclared rule, patch statistics from earlier DINO blocks (L6, L9, and all blocks combined) recover more than half of the KoNViD gap to ResNet-50 ridge, and the block-6 and all-block variants exceed it; block-12 patch statistics do not. The reading is that quality information is present in earlier DINO layers and lost at the last layer. LIVE-VQC rows (exploratory, never fed back) show the same ordering. Sources: [summary](results/posthoc_dino/summary.csv); [comparisons](results/posthoc_dino/comparisons.csv); [LIVE](results/posthoc_dino/live.csv).
- **Part B finding:** mixed. The block-6 patch-mean branch is the most distortion-sensitive DINO branch, which is consistent with Part A, but sensitivity is not monotone with depth and ResNet-50 is more distortion-sensitive than every DINO branch, so raw sensitivity alone does not explain the Part A ranking. The branch-mean summary rule was fixed after Part A and before Part B ran; the qualifying checks were added after Part B and are labelled as such. Sources: [Part B](results/posthoc_dino/part_b.csv); [figure](results/posthoc_dino/part_b_ratio.svg).
- **Limitations:** post-hoc and chosen after Phase 1–3 results; five variants and nine unadjusted comparisons per metric; **every variant-fold, including the original A0, selected the largest alpha in the grid**, and the grid was not extended as predeclared, so all DINO ridge results may be under-regularized and the comparisons could change with a wider grid; the bootstrap covers test-set sampling only.
- **Integrity:** every Phase 1–3 gate-listed hash is unchanged. The six Phase 4a-listed files that differ were all changed by Phase 4b work or the approved decision-chain verifier before this analysis, not by it. Source: [hash check](results/posthoc_dino/hash_check.json).
